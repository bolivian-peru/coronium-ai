Wallet-bound accounts

Every account is an EVM keypair (BIP-39). Same mnemonic works in MetaMask. Lose the API key? Sign a message; get a new one.

Spend caps as law

Server-enforced per-call, per-session, per-day. Your runaway agent can't drain your balance.
